# Walkthrough (SPOILERS)

Puzzles alternate between the two rooms, so both players stay busy.

| # | Lock (room) | Clue lives in… | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Carved cabinet** (Saya): 8 symbol buttons | Rin's crayon staircase: *climb from the bottom, only red, never the broken step* | Eye → Tooth → Knot → Spiral |
| 2 | **Cradle padlock** (Rin): 4 digits | Rin's ledger gives the order; Saya's dolls give the stitch counts (ink tears 4, missing eye 3, long hair 8, red ribbon 6; the blue ribbon doll is a decoy) | **4386** |
| 3 | **Grandfather clock** (Rin): set the time | Saya's pocket watch (from the cabinet). It's turned on its side; *"the eye always looks up"* means the red eye marks 12 | **4:45** |
| 4 | Light the candle (Rin) | Candle (cradle) + matches (clock) | Reveals the handprints and the mirror writing |
| 5 | **Curtain of hair** (Saya) | Scissors from the cabinet | Cut it to reveal the lamp panel |
| 6 | **Lamp panel** (Saya): 4×4 lamps | Rin's handprints, but it's the same wall seen from the other side, so every row is **mirrored** | Rows from Saya's side: `■■□□ / □■□□ / □■■■ / □□□■` |
| 7 | **Vanity letter lock** (Rin): 6 letters | Hair shapes on Rin's mirror + Saya's stitch-chart (from the desk drawer). Watch spiral direction and knot tails | **SISTER** |
| 8 | **The door** (both) | Saya's porcelain tongue says 7·4; Rin's locket says 2·9. *"Outer rings are mine, inner are hers"* | Saya: **7 4 2 9** · Rin: **2 9 7 4** |

Each sister gets a different ending. Read them to each other.
