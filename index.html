a two-player horror escape room

The Wall
Between Us

Two sisters. Two rooms. One wall that is starting to breathe.

Best with headphones · 2 players on 2 devices · ~45–75 min
Contains flashing images, body horror & jump scares (you can turn them off).

Before you go in

  1. You need two players, each on their own screen (phone, tablet or computer).
  2. Each player picks a different sister. Saya wakes in the doll atelier. Rin wakes in the nursery on the other side of the wall.
  3. Get on a voice call (or sit back to back). Don't show each other your screens. Describe what you see.
  4. Almost every lock in your room is opened by something in your sister's room. Talk constantly.
  5. Click anything that looks interesting. Items you pick up go in your pockets at the bottom; click them to inspect or use them.
  6. Stuck? The Hints button gives gentle nudges, then the answer.

Something lives in the walls of this house. It gets bolder the longer you stay.
